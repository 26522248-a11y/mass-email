# Disclosure template

```
Subject: [Security] <short title> in <product>

Hi <team>,

Summary
-------
<1-3 câu: lớp lỗi, điều kiện, tác động>

Affected
--------
Product/version: <...>
Endpoint: <MX/host/API>
First observed: <date/time UTC>
Reporter: <name/handle>, <contact>

Impact
------
<ai bị ảnh hưởng, cần precondition gì, scale>

Reproduction (minimum)
----------------------
1. <command / packet sequence>
2. <expected vs actual>
3. <evidence file>

Evidence
--------
<log, pcap, screenshot; không kèm dữ liệu người thật>

Suggested fix
-------------
<ngắn, cụ thể>

Disclosure
----------
I plan to publish a technical write-up after 90 days or when a fix is
available, whichever comes first. I will not share details with third
parties before that. Please confirm receipt and a tentative timeline.

Safe harbor
-----------
This testing was limited to <domain/mailbox> owned by me / my lab.
No production user data was accessed.

Thanks,
<name>
```
